## Tile 1: Jaeger-LeCoultre

**tile title:** Jaeger-LeCoultre
**tile supporting text:** Immersive pop-up experience bringing 190 years of watchmaking to life in Manhattan.
**tile cover image:** Use a hero image from the Jaeger-LeCoultre activation (exterior pavilion at Hudson Yards at dusk, gold-lit interior visible through glass).

### Lightbox content

**headline:** An icon brought to life through an immersive pop-up experience in the heart of Manhattan

Jaeger-LeCoultre is one of the most storied names in haute horlogerie. When they set out to bring the legacy of the Reverso to New York City, they needed more than a pop-up. They needed a world.

Over the course of a 10-day activation at Hudson Yards' Public Square & Gardens, Futureman designed and produced "Reverso Stories," an immersive, multi-chapter journey spanning nine decades of craftsmanship, innovation, and design. The experience guided visitors through four narrative chapters: Story of an Icon, Story of Style & Design, Story of Innovation, and Story of Craftsmanship. Each brought to life through rarely seen archive timepieces, contemporary high jewelery pieces, and live artisan demonstrations including the decorative craft of perlage, giving the public an intimate look at a process typically hidden behind Manufacture walls.

As part of Jaeger-LeCoultre's Made of Makers program, Futureman built a dedicated sensory environment for an exclusive collaboration with Scent Artist Nicolas Bonneville, featuring three bespoke fragrances and an interactive ingredient identification game. The activation also included a nomadic 1931 Cafe, a branded hospitality moment within the larger experience.

The activation served as the global stage for the launch of the limited-edition Reverso Tribute Chronograph, tying product storytelling directly to experiential design in a city whose Art Deco DNA mirrors the watch's own origins.

Free and open to the public with guided tours, "Reverso Stories" transformed a commercial plaza into a cultural destination proving that when heritage meets bold creative execution, the result isn't just a pop-up. It's a pilgrimage.

**Why this matters for IonQ:** This project required translating a deeply technical craft (haute horlogerie) into an experience that resonated with audiences ranging from collectors to casual passersby. The same challenge IonQ faces: making complex, precise technology feel accessible and compelling to visitors who range from quantum PhDs to Members of Congress. The institutional, editorial quality of this activation is the aesthetic register IonQ is looking for.

## Tile 2: LifeWave

**tile title:** LifeWave
**tile supporting text:** $5MM Welcome Center with interactive kiosks, immersive OLED environments, and a full visitor journey.
**tile cover image:** Use an image of the OLED hyperspace tunnel interior or the main Welcome Center environment.

### Lightbox content

**headline:** Interactive kiosks, immersive OLED content, dynamic sound design, all programmed in 18 languages

LifeWave set out to build a $5MM Welcome Center anchored around a Starship-Enterprise-level experience. They didn't just need vendors. They needed a creative and production partner who could architect the full journey end-to-end. They brought in Futureman.

Over four months, Futureman worked shoulder-to-shoulder with LifeWave and Original Syndicate, concepting, designing, and engineering an integrated universe that would transport every visitor the moment they stepped inside.

Futureman led the creative development of a fleet of interactive kiosks that put LifeWave's full product ecosystem at guests' fingertips. Built with a custom responsive system, the kiosks deliver lightning-fast interactivity and the ability to instantly transform into any of 18 languages, reprogramming the entire experience based on who steps up to the screen.

To extend the journey, the video and 3D teams engineered a hero "hyperspace tunnel" spanning three seamless OLED screens. Futureman curated two decades of archival footage, designed cinematic 3D environments, and crafted an immersive narrative moment composed of a multi-zone soundscape that shifts across four distinct environments within the Welcome Center. The result: a truly transportive world that envelops visitors in the LifeWave story the moment they arrive.

**Why this matters for IonQ:** This is the closest analog to the IonQ EBC in Futureman's portfolio. A permanent visitor center, built end-to-end: interactive kiosks, immersive environments, multi-zone content, dynamic sound design, and a visitor journey architected from first step to last. The production capability demonstrated here (custom interactive systems, large-format OLED content, spatial audio, multi-language support) maps directly to what IonQ's EBC requires. Different aesthetic, same production muscle.

## Tile 3: United Nations Foundation

**tile title:** United Nations Foundation
**tile supporting text:** A decade-long digital partnership evolving the brand and platform for a global institution.
**tile cover image:** Use the laptop mockup showing the UNF website, or the SDG grid layout.

### Lightbox content

**headline:** New style for a legacy organization

The United Nations Foundation originally connected with Futureman over a decade ago when they needed help launching their "Nothing but Net" Campaign. Futureman delivered, and then some. That engagement led to a full rebuild of UNF's corporate website, one that streamlined their mission and values and one that strategically architected their campaigns from their initiatives.

Through a robust discovery and planning phase, Futureman worked to evolve the brand and transform both audience and organizational perception. The team was able to uncover key inputs and insights that helped create an intuitive and sensible information architecture for what UNF does and what they care about. This, coupled with a new visual design and custom website backend, enabled the site to continue to stay fresh with breaking news and new content, and continues to provide their audiences with a simplified way to engage with the organization.

A decade in, Futureman continues to refine and grow UNF's digital presence, with another full refresh on the docket for 2026.

**Why this matters for IonQ:** This relationship demonstrates two things IonQ should care about. First, Futureman's experience working with institutions whose audiences include heads of state, diplomats, and policy leaders, the same profile that will walk through IonQ's DC EBC. Second, the longevity: Futureman doesn't hand off and disappear. A decade of continuous partnership, evolving content as the organization evolves, is exactly the kind of relationship a content-forward EBC requires as IonQ's story, use cases, and audience continue to develop.
