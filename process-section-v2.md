# Process

Every engagement begins with Discovery and ends with a commissioned, signed-off experience. Five phases. Defined deliverables and a formal sign-off gate at each one.

## Phase 1: Discovery

- **Technical SME interviews**
  - Focused sessions with 3 to 5 quantum scientists, product leads, solutions engineers
  - Extracting the stories that matter: real customer problems, classical computing failures, use cases working now vs. a year out
  - Goal: content that is genuinely IonQ's and no one else's
- **Audience mapping**
  - Concrete audience profile matrix across the full visitor range (Members of Congress to quantum PhDs)
  - Who comes in, what they know, what they need to leave believing, what success looks like per type
- **Narrative framework session**
  - Working session with IonQ Marketing
  - Aligning on core story spine: the single feeling every visitor leaves with, hierarchy of messages, content architecture decisions
- **Content audit**
  - Structured review of existing technical materials and sales resources
  - Not to reuse, but to understand what has been said, in what register, and where the gaps are
- **Site and AV alignment**
  - Early coordination with Seneca Group, Perkins Will, and facilities vendors
  - Confirming screen specs, spatial flow, and technical constraints before anything gets designed

## Phase 2: Content & Design

- **Scriptwriting and narrative development**
  - Every content piece (video, ambient loop, interactive sequence, use case module) starts with a script or written brief
  - Reviewed by IonQ Marketing and at least one technical SME before visualization begins
- **Visual language definition**
  - The aesthetic system governing how IonQ's quantum story looks and moves
  - Color, typography, spatial metaphors, visual treatment of data and abstraction
  - Design is credibility: too commercial undermines the tech, too academic loses the room
- **Content architecture mapping**
  - Master document mapping every content piece to every surface in the EBC
  - Which screen plays what, in which mode, how modules connect
  - At the Large tier: visitor personalization logic and audience-specific content queuing

## Phase 3: Production

- **3D and CGI development**
  - Ion trap visualizations, quantum circuit animations, use case environment builds, large-format ambient loops
  - All custom developed, not stock, not templated
  - Visual language document governs every render decision
- **Motion design**
  - Kinetic logic of the full content system: transitions, data animation, response to time and presence
  - Ambient content built to sustain extended loops without becoming predictable
- **Interactive development** *(Medium and Large)*
  - Sensor integration, dome experience logic, presence-detection triggers, gesture interaction systems
  - Visitor personalization at Large tier
  - Built in direct coordination with AV integration vendors
- **Footage and AI integration**
  - B-roll and AI-generated content integrated and graded to match the overall visual language
  - One seamless system, not a patchwork

## Phase 4: QA & Review

- **Internal QA**
  - Visual quality, technical accuracy, motion integrity, cross-surface consistency
  - All reviewed before IonQ sees anything
  - Interactive content: functional testing of all triggers, state changes, and edge cases (simultaneous visitors, skipped modules, sensor dropout)
- **Client review rounds**
  - Two structured rounds with IonQ Marketing and technical SMEs
  - Content accuracy is non-negotiable for a company whose visitors include PhDs
  - Revision vs. scope change defined upfront
- **Cross-surface review**
  - Content reviewed at actual scale on actual formats
  - File formats, color profiles, and playback specs confirmed against AV integration requirements

## Phase 5: Installation & Handoff

- **AV vendor coordination**
  - Content delivered in correct formats, mapped to correct screens
  - Media server configuration and playback setup included
- **On-site presence**
  - At least one Futureman lead on-site during installation
  - Catching issues that only surface in the real environment (calibrated monitor vs. large-format LED under real lighting)
- **Content loading, testing, and calibration**
  - All assets loaded and verified on the playback system
  - Full end-to-end walkthrough of every tour mode (self-guided, presenter-led, and at Large tier, personalized visitor tracks)
  - Every sensor trigger, content transition, and interactive state tested
  - Screen brightness, audio levels, sensor sensitivity tuned to actual space conditions
- **Presenter training**
  - Working session with IonQ staff on triggering modules, navigating content tracks, recovering from technical issues mid-tour
- **Handoff documentation**
  - Complete package: content asset library, playback system docs, content update protocols, AV vendor contacts
  - IonQ Marketing able to operate and refresh the EBC independently from day one

30-day post-launch support window included. Refresh and retainer engagements scoped separately after that.
